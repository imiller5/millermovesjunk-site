Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you serve?

We serve Edgewater, MD and surrounding areas within 1 hour.

How does pricing work?

We charge based on volume, ease of access, and item weight. Base rates and add-on fees are available on our pricing page.

What types of junk do you remove?

We remove typical household junk such as furniture, appliances, trash, miscellaneous household items, yard waste, large appliances (refrigerator, washer, dryer), bulk items (tub, hot tub), yard waste.

Do you remove everything?

No. We do not remove hazardous waste or materials requiring special permits.

Is there an extra charge for stairs?

Yes, $30 per flight of stairs. Please see our pricing sheet for all other extra fees.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Square (credit/debit, Apple Pay, Google Pay), Zelle, and cash. Payment is due upon completion unless otherwise agreed.

Do you do pickup window times?

Yes. Pickup windows are 2 hours, we will text or call to give you a 30 minute heads-up when we are on the way.

Do I need to be home during pickup?

Not always. For curbside pickups or prepaid arrangements, we may be able to complete service without you present.

Do you offer same-day service?

Yes, based on availability. Contact us early in the day for best chances.

What’s the fastest way to get a quote?

Text or email us photos of your junk for a faster estimate or fill out our intake form.

Do I have to leave items curbside?

No, we are full service. However, there may be an extra charge for long stairs and/or long carries.

Can I pick and choose different items throughout the property?

Yes.

How does the mielage fee work?

We provide 20 mile courtesy miles round-trip. There is a $1.25 charge per mile thereafter. Mileage is calculated from our facility in Edgewater to your property, then from your property to the nearest dump and back to our facility. The average mileage fee for a property about 15 miles away from our facility is about $35.

Do you provide any discounts?

Yes. We provide a 15% senior discount, as well as a 10% military/veterans discount.

Any referral bonuses?

Yes. The referee will receive $20 off their next haul and the customer being referred will also receive $10 off their first haul.
